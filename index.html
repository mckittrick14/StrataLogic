Origin Story

Built for Fence-Line Vigilance & Industrial Accountability

StrataOS Analytics was conceived out of a crucial need: offering communities, landowners, and facilities verifiable, tamper-resistant data along industrial perimeters. As data centers, power plants, and heavy infrastructure expand into suburban and rural buffer zones, monitoring localized environmental impact becomes paramount.

By deploying industrial-grade ASA weather-sealed enclosures with custom modular electronics, our platforms provide uncompromised data integrity—giving stakeholders clear visibility into hydrology, decibels, air quality, and light spill.

⚡ Core Platform Architecture

Custom ASA Hardware Enclosures

UV-resistant, weather-sealed, bolt-free exterior designs built for years of outdoor exposure.

Open Telemetry Standards

Seamless export to InfluxDB, MQTT, or Grafana dashboards without proprietary lock-in.

Edge Sensor Processing

Local signal filtering and onboard validation ensure accurate, low-noise field measurements.
