Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

StrataLogic ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect information collected through our website, telemetry devices, and IoT edge platform.

2. Information We Collect

    Telemetry & Environmental Data: Data collected by field hardware (e.g., microclimate metrics, air quality, luminosity, noise levels, and flow telemetry).
  • Contact Information: Information provided voluntarily via contact or inquiry forms (such as name, email address, and phone number).
  • Technical Logs: Server logs, IP addresses, and basic usage analytics gathered automatically when visiting our website.

3. How We Use Information

We use the data collected to:

  • Deliver real-time telemetry metrics and operational alerts.
  • Improve our hardware performance, firmware reliability, and cloud platform interfaces.
  • Respond directly to inquiries and provide customer support.

4. Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or agricultural telemetry data to third parties. We employ physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to protect sensor streams and user credentials from unauthorized access.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us via our main website form.
